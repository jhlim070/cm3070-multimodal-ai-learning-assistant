© 2024 Singapore Institute of Management Pte LtdSeparation of Concerns 
➢Separation of Concerns (SoC) is a fundamental principle in 
software engineering and design aimed at breaking down 
complex systems into smaller, more manageable parts. 
➢The goal is to organize a system’s components in a way that 
each part addresses a single concern, or a cohesive aspect of 
functionality, rather than mixing multiple concerns together. 
➢This approach enhances modularity, maintainability, and 
scalability of software systems.

© 2024 Singapore Institute of Management Pte LtdSeparation of Concerns
Concerns : In the context of software, a “concern” represents a 
specific aspect of functionality, behaviour, or responsibility within the 
system. For example, concerns might include user interface 
presentation, data storage and retrieval, business logic processing, 
error handling, security, etc. Each concern represents a cohesive 
aspect that can be managed independently.Separation : The word “separation” emphasizes the act of isolating or 
distinguishing different concerns within a system. By separating 
concerns, software engineers aim to create clearer boundaries and 
reduce the interdependence between different parts of the system.

© 2024 Singapore Institute of Management Pte LtdBenefits of Separation of Concern
➢Separation of concerns is a Node.js architecture that ensures code readability, 
easy refactoring, and good code collaboration.
➢Using the separation of concerns principle, you can ensure that the final system 
is stable and maintainable.
➢The principle of separation of concerns ensures that components are not 
duplicated, making the system easier to maintain and refactor.
➢Separation of Concerns principle aids in code reusability. This reduces 
maintenance costs and time by making it simple to determine where a fault is 
coming from and how to isolate it from the entire system.

© 2024 Singapore Institute of Management Pte LtdRouting in Node.js
➢Routing defines how the client requests are handled by the 
application endpoints. Routing in Node refers to the process of 
determining how an application responds to client requests to 
different endpoints (URLs). 
➢In a web application, these endpoints typically correspond to different 
pages or functionalities within the application. 
➢Node.js, along with frameworks like Express, provides a way to define 
routes, which are responsible for handling specific HTTP requests and 
sending back appropriate responses.

© 2024 Singapore Institute of Management Pte LtdRoutes management
In Node.js, managing routes directly in a main.js  file is a common 
approach for small applications. 
However, as the application grows in complexity, developers often 
switch to modular routing to improve the organization and 
maintainability of the codebase .

© 2024 Singapore Institute of Management Pte LtdAll Routes in main.js  (Non -modular 
approach)
Pros:
▪Simplicity: Easy to set up for small projects with fewer routes.
▪Quick to implement: No need for additional files or complex structure.
Cons:
▪Scalability issues: As the app grows, the main.js  file becomes hard to 
manage.
▪Harder to maintain: All logic is in one file, making it difficult to isolate 
features or debug.
▪Reduced readability: Large file with mixed concerns (routes, middleware, 
etc.) becomes less readable.

© 2024 Singapore Institute of Management Pte LtdModular Routing
Pros :
▪Better organization: Routes are grouped by features (e.g., auth.js , 
user.js ), improving clarity and structure.
▪Scalability: Easily extendable as the app grows by adding new route 
modules.
▪Easier to maintain: Each module is focused on a specific concern, which 
improves code readability and organization.
▪Improved testing: Each module can be tested independently.
Cons :
▪Complexity: Additional setup required (more files and imports).
▪Overhead: For small projects, splitting routes may introduce unnecessary 
complexity.
